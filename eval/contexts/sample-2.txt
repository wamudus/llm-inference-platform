Nimbus N1 and N2 are both dual-socket compute nodes.

N1:
- 2 × PCIe 5.0 x16
- 4 × PCIe 5.0 x8
- 800W power supply

N2:
- 4 × PCIe 5.0 x16
- 1200W power supply

Selection notes:
- If the workload needs four full-width GPUs, choose N2.
- If the workload needs two GPUs and more x8 expansion, N1 is sufficient.
- Both nodes support the same CPU and memory configuration.
